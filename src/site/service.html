Plastic and Aesthetic Surgery

Specialization Our Treatments

Every treatment begins with listening. A precise diagnosis, a plan designed around your goals, and modern surgical techniques for results that look and feel natural.
Rhinoplasty (Nose Reshaping)
Refined, natural-looking nose reshaping that harmonises your facial features while preserving easy breathing.
Rhinoplasty is planned around your unique facial proportions. Dr. Fatema Mirza reshapes bone and cartilage with precision to smooth a dorsal hump, refine the tip or correct breathing problems — always aiming for a balanced, natural result rather than an operated look.
Read More
Breast Augmentation
Safe, personalised breast enhancement using implants or your own fat, sized to your body and your goals.
Breast augmentation restores volume and symmetry after pregnancy, weight loss or simply by choice. Implant type, size and placement are chosen with you during a relaxed consultation, and modern techniques keep incisions as discreet as possible with a faster, more comfortable recovery.
Read More
Liposuction & Body Contouring
Targeted removal of stubborn fat from the waist, hips, thighs and arms to reveal smoother, natural contours.
When diet and exercise stop working, liposuction sculpts the areas that resist. Using a gentle tumescent technique, stubborn fat is removed from the abdomen, flanks, thighs or arms to restore natural proportions — with even, smooth results and minimal downtime.
Read More
Facelift & Neck Lift
Lifting and tightening that refreshes the face and neck while keeping every expression completely natural.
A modern facelift repositions sagging tissue rather than just pulling skin. The result is a rested, younger version of you — a defined jawline, softened jowls and a smoother neck — with scars hidden around the ears and hairline.
Read More
Tummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty)
Removes stretched skin and repairs separated muscles to restore a firm, flat abdomen after pregnancy or weight loss.
A tummy tuck addresses what exercise cannot: loose skin, stretch-marked tissue below the navel and separated abdominal muscles. The repair also narrows the waist, and the scar sits low enough to hide under swimwear.
Read More
Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)
Brighter, more open eyes — removes drooping upper-lid skin and under-eye bags with fine, hidden incisions.
Eyelid surgery gently removes excess skin and fat pads that make the eyes look tired. Incisions follow the natural lid crease, so they fade into it — the change looks refreshing rather than different.
Read More
Breast Lift & Breast Reduction
Restores a youthful breast position and comfortable proportions while preserving natural shape and sensation.
Whether lifting after pregnancy or reducing for comfort, the breast is reshaped around your own tissue. Size, position and symmetry are planned to your frame, relieving shoulder and back pain while creating a natural, proportionate silhouette.
Read More
Hair Transplant
Natural hairline restoration using your own follicles, with dense, permanent growth and no visible scarring.
Using FUE technique, individual follicles are taken from the donor area and placed to rebuild the hairline and crown. Design matters as much as density — the new hairline is drawn to match your face and age for an undetectable result.
Read More
Gynecomastia Surgery
Restores a flatter, firmer male chest by removing excess gland and fat, through discreet hidden incisions.
Gynecomastia surgery removes the gland tissue and fat that give the chest a rounded appearance. Most men return to work within days, wearing a compression garment, and the chest looks naturally masculine in and out of clothing.
Read More
Botox & Dermal Fillers
Quick, non-surgical softening of lines and gentle restoration of facial volume — done in minutes, no downtime.
Injectable treatments smooth frown lines, crow's feet and forehead creases, and restore cheek and lip volume lost with age. Dosage is deliberately conservative — you still look like yourself, just fresher.
Read More
Otoplasty (Ear Surgery)
Sets prominent ears closer to the head and reshapes them — often done in childhood, always confidence-building.
Otoplasty reshapes ear cartilage through an incision hidden behind the ear. Protruding or asymmetrical ears are corrected permanently, and adults can return to normal life within a week.
Read More
Mommy Makeover
A combined, staged plan — tummy tuck, breast and body contouring — to restore your pre-pregnancy figure safely.
A mommy makeover combines the procedures you need — commonly breast lift or augmentation with a tummy tuck and liposuction — into one recovery whenever it is safe. Every plan is staged around your health, family time and goals.
Read More
Scar Revision & Keloid Care
Refines old, thickened or stretched scars with surgical and non-surgical techniques so they blend with the surrounding skin.
From keloid treatment to fine scar revision, Dr. Fatema Mirza combines surgical refinement, steroid therapy and silicone-based care to make scars flatter, paler and far less noticeable.
Read More
PRP & Skin Rejuvenation
Uses growth factors from your own blood to boost skin quality, hair growth and healing — natural, gentle and safe.
PRP therapy harnesses your body's own growth factors to improve skin texture, soften fine lines and support hair restoration. Sessions are quick, with no downtime, and results build naturally over a few weeks.

About Dr. Fatema Mirza

Dr. Fatema Mirza is a plastic and aesthetic surgeon devoted to restoring form, function and confidence — from complex reconstructive surgery to refined aesthetic procedures.

Her practice has a special focus on neurofibroma and plexiform neurofibroma management, along with skin and soft-tissue reconstruction. Every consultation begins with listening, and every treatment plan is built around the person in front of her — never a template.
